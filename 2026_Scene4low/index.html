SCENE4LOW

Amodal 4D Scene Completion

Under submission to CVPR 2026

Abstract

We study amodal 4D scene completion: Given partial point clouds of a scene at one or two time steps, predict its complete colored geometry together with an amodal scene flow that maps every completed point, visible or occluded, between time steps. Existing methods address this only in pieces: amodal reconstruction handles static snapshots, 4D generation handles single objects, and scene flow covers only observed points. We present SCENE4LOW, a generative model that completes geometry, color, and motion jointly within one 3D representation. Because it operates directly on point clouds, completion is decoupled from depth and pose estimation, and a single model works with monocular depth, multi-view reconstruction, or depth sensors without retraining. We adopt sparse-convolutional tokenizers encode the point distribution within each voxel into spatially aligned shape and color latents. In this latent space, an occupancy transformer proposes missing structure, a diffusion transformer completes the shapes from two frames jointly, and a cellwise deformation head predicts motion for every completed cell, including surfaces never observed. We train on ∼150K static scenes, objects, and animated assets with simulated partial observations. SCENE4LOW achieves state-of-the-art amodal completion on SCRREAM and ScanNet++, reduces scene-flow error on Hi4D from 1.91 to 1.17 cm compared to SAM 3D Body, and matches or outperforms dedicated 4D generators on ActionBench.
