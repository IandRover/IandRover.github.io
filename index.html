Chia-Hsiang Kao

Email / CV / Google Scholar

I am a 3rd-year CS PhD student at Cornell and a Cornell Foundational AI PhD Fellow, advised by Prof. Bharath Hariharan. My research focuses on inferring the hidden dynamics of real-world interactions, spanning vision-language reasoning, visual-symbolic learning (video-to-code), and physics simulation from video. I am also excited about applications in remote sensing and biomedicine.

I am currently a Student Researcher at Meta and was previously an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon. Before Cornell, I obtained my M.D. in Taiwan.
